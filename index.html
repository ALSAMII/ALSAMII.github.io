Door

    Nothing behind that door yet.

      There’s something you’ve never said out loud. These are short noir novellas about people who finally do—and what it costs them.

      Every book asks the same question: how does a person get out of their own head?

      Door How they got out.

      Room Where they ended up.

      Key The one thing that did it.

      Three ways in

      Author's Notes

      Some thoughts can't be said out loud without changing how the room sees you afterward. So they don't get said.

      Not shameful thoughts. The partly true ones. Everyone keeps one, unsaid, for years—and unsaid is what keeps it alive. Sooner or later it wants a body that isn't yours. I give it one.

      Nothing here resolves. I'm suspicious of anyone whose does.

      Bring your ugliest thought. There's a chair for it.

      — Chew Z
