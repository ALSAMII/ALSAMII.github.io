    Door

      Nothing matches that.

        Read the story — PDF

        For years I read other people’s stories and lived in worlds someone else had already decided the shape of. Then a drug took the walls off one night and I made my own — planner, participant, whole production crew — and I have not been a guest since, so I finish them here.

        Short noir novellas about people who finally say it out loud. Every book works the same way — a door out of the mind, a room on the other side, and the key that opened it.

        Author's Notes

        Some thoughts can't be said out loud without changing how the room sees you afterward. So they don't get said.

        Not shameful thoughts. The partly true ones. Everyone keeps one, unsaid, for years—and unsaid is what keeps it alive. Sooner or later it wants a body that isn't yours. I give it one.

        Nothing here resolves. I'm suspicious of anyone whose does.

        Bring your ugliest thought. There's a chair for it.

        — Chew Z
