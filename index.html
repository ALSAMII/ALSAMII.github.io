    About

    Chew Z writes dark literary novellas set in a shared world called the Reach—slow-burning, noir-inflected, grounded in a world neither better nor worse than this one. Each book puts one altered state at its center—a drug, a ritual, a fast, a song—and follows a small cast into what that state makes undeniable. Published by Roya Publication, numbered in sequence.

    Author's Notes

    I write short books about the thoughts I can't finish out loud.

    Mostly I'm interested in what comfort costs us, what the body knows that the mind can talk itself out of, and how thin the line is between a group that protects you and one that owns you. I don't write dystopias—the world in these books is exactly as compromised as this one. I don't offer answers either. I'm suspicious of anyone who would. I'd rather hand you the question and let you set it down when you're finished.

    — Chew Z
